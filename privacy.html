Privacy notice

A starter page to help explain how your real school website handles information.

This is a template, not legal advice or a final privacy policy. Have it reviewed and replace it with a policy that reflects your school's actual practices and applicable law.

Information you provide

This demo website includes contact and admissions forms for presentation. They do not transmit or store submissions. Before connecting a form, explain what information is collected, why it is needed, who receives it and how long it is retained.

Children's information

Schools may handle sensitive information about children and families. Collect only what is necessary, restrict access, use appropriate safeguards and obtain any required permissions. Do not request private student records through an unsecured website form.

Images and embedded services

Sample photographs load from external image URLs and Google Fonts may be requested from Google servers. Replace demo images with properly licensed, school-approved images and review any third-party services used by the finished site.

Cookies and analytics

This template does not intentionally set analytics cookies. If you add analytics, embedded media or other tracking, update the notice and configure any consent mechanisms that your legal obligations require.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the school using the verified details on the Contact page. Replace this example page with a reviewed policy before public launch.
