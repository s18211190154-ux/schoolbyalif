Accessibility

The design aims to keep school information readable and usable across phones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers.

⌨

Keyboard and navigation

Interactive elements have visible focus styles, and the site uses simple navigation and labelled form fields. Continue testing all pages without a mouse.

◐

Readable themes

Visitors can switch between light and dark themes. Check contrast and legibility after replacing colours, school logos or photography.

↔

Responsive layout

Layouts adapt to smaller screens, but real-device testing remains important before launch.

〰

Reduced motion

The page design respects the operating system's reduced-motion preference so nonessential animations can be minimised.
