Our story

A school shaped by a simple belief.

Every child deserves to be known, encouraged and challenged in ways that help them flourish. School by Alif is a sample school concept built around that belief and a commitment to meaningful learning.

Our community brings together students, families, teachers and local partners. We aim to make school a place where differences are respected, questions are welcomed and effort is celebrated.

  • Communication, collaboration and critical thinking
  • Creative and physical activities that build confidence
  • A partnership with families based on trust and respect
