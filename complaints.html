Before you begin

A process built around fairness.

Schools should offer clear steps for receiving, reviewing and responding to concerns. Replace this sample guidance with the institution's approved complaints policy and reporting contacts.

01

Explain the concern

Share what happened, roughly when it happened and what outcome you are hoping for. Include only information needed to understand the issue.

02

Use the correct channel

For urgent safeguarding or immediate safety risks, use the school's official urgent reporting route or local emergency services. Do not rely on this demo page.

03

Allow a review

A real school should explain who receives a report, expected response times and how information is kept confidential.

Privacy: This page is public and its form is not connected to anyone. Do not include names of children, sensitive details, or information requiring immediate action.
