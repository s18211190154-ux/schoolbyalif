Complaints & feedback

A respectful route for suggestions and concerns. This page is a preview only; submissions do not reach the school.

How to share a concern

STEP 01

Explain what happened

Describe the issue clearly and include only the details needed to understand it.

STEP 02

Choose the right route

Use a verified school contact route for private concerns. For urgent safety matters, follow the school's official safeguarding procedures.

STEP 03

Allow a review

A real system should explain who receives a report, how it is handled and when a response may arrive.
