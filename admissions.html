Getting prepared

Things you may be asked to provide.

The official requirements depend on your school, class level and local regulations. Use this list as a starting point only.

  • Student's birth certificate or accepted ID
  • Recent photographs, if required
  • Previous school report or transfer certificate, if applicable
  • Parent or guardian contact details
  • Any relevant medical or learning support information, shared securely
Do not send sensitive documents through this demo website. Request the school's official secure application channel.
