Before you contact the school

Where can I find school notices?

Use the Notices page. Its public examples are loaded from JSON files in the repository data folder.

Can I get student grades or attendance here?

Not on this public website. Real student records require a secure system with individual sign-in and appropriate access controls.

Does this contact form send an email?

No. This is a front-end preview. It shows a confirmation message but does not transmit or save submissions.
